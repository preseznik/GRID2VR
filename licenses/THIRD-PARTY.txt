GRID2VR 0.1.0 distribution components

The package includes the original notices for MinHook, Khronos OpenXR,
PowerShell, Microsoft .NET, CPython, NumPy, Pillow and their bundled components.
Runtime licenses and third-party notices are also retained inside payload/.
Microsoft Visual C++ redistributables are included in their original form.

GRID2VR.exe also contains the GRID2VR track converter (custom tracks), built
from EgoEngineLibrary source revision f3fe9ee0f6e8379c64bc4a29e1d1b586bba5d1c1
(MIT, EGO-MIT.txt) with one local fix (tools/ego-sheet-materials.patch). It
uses the same dependency versions listed above, plus
CommunityToolkit.Diagnostics 8.4.2: MIT; original notices are under
converter/communitytoolkit.diagnostics. Converted tracks are built on the
player's PC from their own copy of the source game; no track content is included.

Original package metadata is included under converter/ for attribution and
source repository identification. Upstream license texts for BCnEncoder,
ZstdSharp and SharpGLTF were obtained from the commits recorded in that
metadata; K4os from its 1.3.8 release tag.

No GRID 2 executables, textures, camera assets or player save files are included.
Local camera/session assets are generated from the player's own installed game.
